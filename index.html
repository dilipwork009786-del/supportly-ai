How Supportly AI could work

A simple starting point for an AI-powered customer support product.

STEP 01

Understand the business

Gather approved information such as FAQs, shipping policies, opening hours, and product details.

STEP 02

Generate helpful replies

Use an AI model to help draft answers grounded in the business's own information.

STEP 03

Keep people in control

Escalate unclear, sensitive, or account-specific questions to a human instead of guessing.
